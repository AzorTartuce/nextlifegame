## 2. Summary

- In this version the game **does not collect personal data** and **does not send information** to any server of ours.

## 3. What is stored on your device

We use local storage (localStorage) to keep:

- your in-game life save (characters, fictional money, choices);
- your chosen language;
- your age confirmation and acknowledgement of the content notice;
- game settings (for example Destiny Mode and the ribbon collection).

This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the site/app data or uninstalling the game. Character names and stories are made up or typed by you; we recommend not typing real personal data.

## 4. Age confirmation
